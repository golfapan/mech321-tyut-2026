IDEAS THAT BECAME ENGINEERING TOOLS

A Short History of Control

Eight milestones behind the tools we use today.

从微分方程到传递函数，再到状态空间：这些工具为什么出现，又让我们多做了什么？

Selected contributions, not a list of sole inventors. Dates mark representative developments. Diagrams are modern teaching illustrations.

  1. 186801

    Feedback becomes a mathematical problem

    James Clerk Maxwell 麦克斯韦

    In “On Governors”, Maxwell analysed a speed governor with differential equations and asked when its motion would settle rather than grow.

    从“装置能调速”走向“方程能判断它是否稳定”。

    Physical model → differential equation → stability

    In MECH321 · Lectures 1, 3–4

  2. 1880s–1920s02

    From differential equations to algebra

    Oliver Heaviside · John R. Carson 赫维赛德 · 卡森

    Heaviside developed operational methods for circuits; Carson helped establish and extend their mathematical basis. These tools helped prepare the input–output language of transfer functions.

    先把微分关系变成代数关系，才更容易用输入与输出的比值描述系统。

    Zero initial conditions: G(s) = Y(s) / U(s)

    In MECH321 · Lectures 2–3

  3. 1927 / 193403

    Negative feedback becomes a practical design tool

    Harold S. Black 哈罗德·布莱克

    Black developed the negative-feedback amplifier: sacrifice some gain to reduce distortion and make amplification less sensitive to component variation. The idea dates to 1927; his major paper appeared in 1934.

    反馈早已有之；这里的突破是把负反馈做成可靠、实用的放大器设计。

    Unity negative feedback: T(s) = G(s) / [1 + G(s)]

    In MECH321 · Lecture 7

  4. 193204

    Judge closed-loop stability from the open loop

    Harry Nyquist 奈奎斯特

    Nyquist connected the open-loop frequency response with closed-loop stability, including systems with unstable open-loop poles.

    还没有闭合回路，就能从开环频率响应判断闭环是否稳定。

    L(s) → encirclements of −1 → closed-loop stability

    In MECH321 · Lecture 8

  5. 1930s–194505

    Frequency response becomes a design language

    Hendrik W. Bode 伯德

    Bode developed logarithmic magnitude-and-phase methods and gain/phase-margin ideas; his 1945 book systematised frequency-domain analysis and design.

    Bode 图不仅是画曲线，还让我们读出带宽、稳定裕度和设计取舍。

    Magnitude: 20 log₁₀|G(jω)| dB · phase: ∠G(jω)

    In MECH321 · Lectures 5, 8

  6. 194806

    Transfer functions become a shared engineering method

    Gordon S. Brown · Donald P. Campbell 布朗 · 坎贝尔

    Their textbook Principles of Servomechanisms helped organise transfer functions, feedback and dynamic design into a method engineers could learn and reuse across applications.

    传递函数不是某个人突然发明的；关键还在于把已有工具整合为可教、可用的工程方法。

    Model → G(s) → block diagram → analysis and design

    In MECH321 · Lectures 3, 6–7

  7. 194807

    See how feedback gain moves the poles

    Walter R. Evans 埃文斯

    Evans introduced root locus: trace the closed-loop poles as gain changes, linking a design parameter directly to stability and transient behaviour.

    改变增益，极点怎样走？极点图把参数调整与时域表现联系起来。

    1 + K G(s)H(s) = 0

    In MECH321 · Lecture 9 · optional

  8. 1960s08

    State space expands what control can describe

    Rudolf E. Kálmán 卡尔曼

    Kalman helped establish modern state-space control through controllability, observability, optimal control and estimation. State variables and state equations had earlier mathematical roots.

    不只看输入与输出，还描述系统内部状态：哪些能控制，哪些能从测量中推知？

    ẋ = Ax + Bu · y = Cx + Du

    In MECH321 · Lecture 6; optimal control and estimation are further study
